# neuroconsumer 0.1.0

* Initial release of `neuroconsumer`.
* Core experimental stimulus DSL: `nc_component()`, `nc_stimulus()`, `nc_add_component()`.
* Declarative experimental manipulation engine: `nc_manipulate()`, `nc_build_variants()`.
* Area of Interest (AOI) derivation engine: `nc_aoi()`, `nc_aoi_from_components()`, `nc_aoi_to_df()`.
* Experimental invariant validation engine: `nc_validate()`.
* Deterministic SVG XML and raster export: `nc_render_svg()`, `nc_export()`.
